Cookie policy

Updated November 25, 2026

The Storeroom app doesn't use cookies. It keeps a few things in your browser's own storage so it can remember who's signed in and keep working without a signal. This page lists what's stored, why, and how to clear it.

Cookies

The app sets no cookies. It has no advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.

What the app keeps in your browser

These stay on your device. When you're signed in, your records also sync to the app's cloud database so your other devices and your team see the same thing.

Services the app connects to

  • Supabase signs you in and stores the shared records, pictures and PDFs. Sign-in uses the tokens above, not cookies.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser downloads the font files from Google, which shares your IP address and browser type with Google. Google Fonts doesn't set cookies.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub may keep server logs, including IP addresses, to run and protect the service.

Camera

The camera turns on only when you select Scan barcode or Scan QR code. The picture is read on your device to find the code, and it isn't saved or sent. Photos you choose to add, like delivery location pictures, are saved like any other record.

How to clear it

  • Log out to remove your sign-in tokens and who's signed in on this device.
  • To remove everything, delete this site's stored data in your browser's settings. Anything that hasn't synced yet is lost, so select Back up first if you need a copy.

Changes

If the app starts storing something new, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Questions

Ask your supervisor or the app's owner. See also the Terms of service.
